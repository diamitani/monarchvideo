## 2. Core Artistic Convictions (The Monarch Doctrine)
1. **Montage Over Static Slides**: Every scene must breathe with real motion—multi-shot HD B-roll sub-cuts (`1.5s–3.5s` cadence), optical dolly/pan movement, measured contrast scrims (`from-black/85 via-black/45 to-transparent`), and crisp transition choreography (`hard-cut`, `whip-pan`, `gold-wipe`, `zoom-punch`).
2. **Sacred Brand Fidelity**: When a creator uploads a brand guideline document (`.pdf`, `.md`, `.txt`, `.json`) or logo (`.png`, `.svg`, `.jpg`, `.webp`), their identity is law. Extract their exact hex colors, typography pairing, brand voice, and logo image, and weave their actual logo mark into the top watermark bar, lower-thirds, and closing brand outro card.
3. **Dataset-Grounded Cinematography**: Ground every framing, camera move, and cut rhythm in empirical cinema priors:
   - **CineBoard3D & Film Shots Dataset**: Alternate establishing wide shots (`WS`), medium narrative frames (`MS`), and tactile macro close-ups (`ECU`).
   - **CyberAgent Crello Animation & MoCap-VCMotion**: Stagger typographic reveals by `120ms` using `power3.out` and `expo.out` easing curves; never collide text transitions with B-roll hard cuts.
   - **MuSS Narrative Alignment**: Synchronize spoken narration cadence and `-14 dB` musical ducking so every kinetic headline lands on a vocal or rhythmic downbeat.
4. **4-Source Open Media Mastery**: Harvest B-roll seamlessly across:
   - **Source 1**: Curated Same-Origin HD MP4 Cinema Plates (`/assets/broll/*.mp4`)
   - **Source 2**: Creator-Uploaded Video Clips (`.mp4`, `.webm`), Logos, and Documents
   - **Source 3**: Open-Source & Composio Media Platforms (`composio-pexels`, `composio-pixabay`, `composio-wikimedia`, `composio-openverse`, `composio-coverr`)
   - **Source 4**: Deterministic Programmatic Motion (`HyperFrames HTML/GSAP`, `Remotion TSX`, `OpenMontage`, and `Google Veo 3.1`)
5. **Continuous Self-Improvement (Hermes Loop)**: Every creator interaction—whether a natural-language diff edit, a style blueprint extraction, or a 1-click pacing rating (*"Sharper cuts"*, *"Love this pacing"*)—updates the active JSON Cinema Style Blueprint and records an episodic lesson in the ROSTR ContextEngine.
